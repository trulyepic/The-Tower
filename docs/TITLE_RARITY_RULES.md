# Title Rarity Rules

Use this file when assigning or reviewing title grades.

## Core Rule

Title rarity should follow `milestone weight`, not raw loot power.

- items:
  - judged more by scarcity, combat value, economy, and drop excitement
- titles:
  - judged more by narrative milestone, guild recognition, and climb prestige

## Current Title Grade Guide

- `common`
  - onboarding milestones
  - first-step recognition
  - very early identity markers
- `rare`
  - first real climb milestones
  - early floors that prove the player is no longer just surviving by luck
  - meaningful guild recognition, but still early in the ascent
- `epic`
  - major chapter clears
  - stronger branch-defining outcomes
  - notable hunt or boss achievements beyond the early climb
- `legendary`
  - mythic or endgame recognition
  - major fate-defining clears, endings, or world-defining feats

## Ongoing Rule

Whenever a new title is added or an existing one is regraded:

1. check the floor/story milestone it represents
2. compare that milestone against existing titles
3. keep title rarity progression grounded so early-floor clears do not feel overstated

## Current Callout

- `First Snare Survivor`
  - currently treated as an early-floor milestone title
  - should stay below mid-game prestige titles
