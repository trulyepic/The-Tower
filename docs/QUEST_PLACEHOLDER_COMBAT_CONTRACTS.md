## Placeholder 3: Hunt Raid

### Raid Hunt: Leviathor of the Coiling Deep

Type:
- `hunt`
- `raid`

Role:
- first high-level placeholder for a real raid-class hunt contract

## Biblical Basis

This raid is intentionally inspired by biblical Leviathan rather than a generic fantasy dragon.

Key source traits:
- Leviathan appears in `Job 41` as an untamable armored beast of terror
- `Isaiah 27:1` describes Leviathan as:
  - the fleeing serpent
  - the twisting serpent
  - the dragon in the sea

Why Leviathan is the right base:
- already dragon-adjacent in scripture
- already tied to scale, dread, chaos, and divine judgment
- fits a high-order raid monster much better than a normal beast contract

Behemoth is also a biblical great creature, but it reads more like a land colossus.
For a dragonish raid-hunt fantasy, Leviathan is the stronger foundation.

## World Adaptation

In this world, Leviathor is not simply “the Bible creature pasted into the game.”

It is a Tower-world reinterpretation:
- a deep remnant wyrm
- remembered through damaged chapel texts and black-ledger guild warnings
- treated as a sea-chaos or abyss-chaos thing older than many lower ascent roads

This keeps the biblical inspiration clear while preserving the game’s own setting identity.

## Naming Direction

`Leviathor` is the current placeholder fantasy form.

Goal of the name:
- clearly echo `Leviathan`
- feel like a boss-class monster name used by guild records
- sound like a named raid threat instead of a direct scripture quote

## Raid Identity

High-order monsters like this should be labeled as:
- `Raid Hunt`

Meaning:
- not a normal field contract
- not a simple board kill order
- a named, multi-stage, high-risk target

## Placeholder Combat Shape

- multiple health bars
