# Tamsin Vale Questline

## Identity

- Name:
  - `Tamsin Vale`
- Title:
  - `Thorn Runner`
- Role:
  - corridor salvage scout
  - thorn-lane specialist
  - recurring Floor 2 survival contact

## Arc Purpose

Tamsin exists to make `Floor 2: Thorn Corridor` feel like a lived-in guild problem rather than just another enemy floor.
She introduces the player to corridor attrition, salvage culture, and the kind of practical adventurer work that sits between board contracts and main-story ascent.

## Phase I: Thorn Runner Introduction

### Trigger
- player becomes genuinely ready for Floor 2
- guild notice appears
- Tamsin becomes available in guild records / NPC hall

### First Read Branch
- `I'll bring it back clean.`
  - Tamsin reads the player as steady and professional
  - improves disposition
- `Just mark the pay.`
  - Tamsin still offers work
  - lowers disposition
  - establishes a colder first read

### First Quest
- `Tamsin's Snagline Recovery`

### Phase I Outcome
- player completes the board contract
- player returns to Tamsin in the guild hall
- Tamsin records what the snagline and satchel revealed about the thorn lane
- thread closes cleanly for this phase
