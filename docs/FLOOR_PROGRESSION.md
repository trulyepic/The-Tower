## Core Rule

Each floor should define:

1. `Gate`
2. `Story role`
3. `Mechanical lesson`
4. `Support loop`
5. `Reward step`
6. `What it unlocks next`

If a floor does not clearly advance those six things, it is not ready.

## World Ceiling

- Playable main-story progression runs through `Floor 50`.
- Documented guild and adventurer knowledge runs through `Floor 55`.
- `Floor 55` is the highest confirmed ascent in recorded Tower history.
- Only `two` climbers are known to have reached `Floor 55`.
- Most `S` and `SS` climbers stabilize between `Floors 40-50`.

## Early Arc

### Floor 1: Ashen Threshold (Beginnings)

- Gate:
  - `Rank F`
  - `Level 1+`
- Story role:
  - first real climb
  - establish the guild floor, the tower mood, and early recurring NPC threads
- Mechanical lesson:
  - basic counters
  - visible attrition
  - wave structure
  - first boss trophy
- Support loop:
  - guild services on Floor 1
  - Floor 1 local jobs
  - Bran store access
  - Lyra conditional tower encounter
  - low-cost guild intel
- Reward step:
  - basic remnants
  - first starter weapon chase
  - early sigil/support-item economy
  - `Warden of Sparks Heart`
- Unlocks next:
  - `Rank E` push
  - `Floor 2: Thorn Corridor`
  - stronger crafting expectations

### Floor 2: Thorn Corridor (The First Snare)

- Gate:
  - `Rank E`
  - `Level 5+`
- Story role:
  - first floor that feels deliberate instead of merely dangerous
  - establishes that the Tower changes its logic floor to floor
- Mechanical lesson:
  - bind pressure
  - bleed / thorn attrition
  - recovery denial
  - longer prep decisions
- Support loop:
  - Floor 2 local work
  - `Briar Resin`
  - `Thorn Salve`
  - low-cost early E-rank guild intel
  - `Tamsin Vale` as the first floor-specific guild-side story contact
  - first Tamsin branch that changes how she reads the player before the board contract fully settles
- Reward step:
  - first stronger class weapon chase
  - `Embershard Sigil`
  - Floor 2 remnants for later Seal/Engraving support
- Unlocks next:
  - Floor 2-specific main quest chapter
  - stronger guild intel expectations
  - first true “specialized floor” mindset

## Build Order For Floor 2

Before we call Floor 2 complete, we need these in order:

1. `Main quest progression`
   - chapter text should explicitly move the player from Floor 1 into Floor 2
   - the game should explain why Thorn Corridor matters in the climb

2. `Support loop`
   - floor-local work should clearly feed:
     - `Briar Resin`
     - `Thorn Salve`
     - recovery stock
   - guild intel should reveal:
     - weaknesses
     - optional drops
     - hidden support aids
   - side-story progression should stay staged:
     - Tamsin Phase I complete for now
     - Aldric v1.1 next: urgency presentation + first dark-path breadcrumb

3. `Tower identity`
   - roster, mechanics, and rewards should all reinforce:
     - thorn
     - bind
     - attrition

4. `Reward step`
   - Floor 2 must feel like a visible step beyond Floor 1
   - at least one of these must land clearly:
     - stronger weapon chase
     - stronger sigil reward
     - strong remnant value

5. `Story continuity`
   - at least one NPC, quest, or notice should connect Floor 2 back into the living guild world

## Intel Rule By Floor

- Early floors should not expose every weakness and optional drop immediately.
- Players can learn floor information through:
  - Bran's guild intel ledger
  - board quests
  - trusted adventurer favors
  - floor experience / prior clears

### Early pricing intent

- `Floor 1`
  - cheap
  - acts as onboarding to the intel system
- `Floor 2`
  - still affordable
  - should feel like a worthwhile preparation purchase, not a tax
- `Floors 3-5`
  - begin stepping up in price
  - should reward either planning or relationship-based shortcuts
