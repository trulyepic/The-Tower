# Riven Hale Questline

## Identity

- Name:
  - `Riven Hale`
- Title:
  - `Hall Duelist`
- Role:
  - sanctioned guild duelist
  - recurring E-rank promotion challenger
  - hall-side mirror for what trained adventurer combat looks like

## Arc Purpose

Riven exists to make the E-rank promotion feel like a real guild relationship instead of a one-off fight.
He is not a random opponent.
He is part of the hall circuit that Nyra Sol uses to test whether an adventurer can read another adventurer under pressure.
This duel is a sanctioned promotion test, not a duel to the death.

## Phase I: Sanctioned Duel Introduction

### Trigger
- player reaches the `E -> D` promotion window
- Nyra Sol opens the trial desk
- Riven is introduced as the duel opponent for `Earn The D-Mark`

### Function
- Riven shows the player what happens when an opponent can:
  - drink tonics
  - raise sigils
  - recover from pressure
  - survive a second phase
- this establishes that rank progression is now a relationship with the guild, not only a combat check
- the duel should be talked about in guild language as an official assessment:
  - both fighters are expected to survive
  - the guild is judging composure, recovery, and control
  - the point is promotion readiness, not lethal victory

### Immediate Outcome
- player either defeats Riven or loses to him under guild watch
- the duel result becomes part of the guild record
- Riven remains a hall-side figure after the bout instead of disappearing into generic trial data

## Phase II: Post-Duel Hall Continuity

### Trigger
- the duel has been resolved
- Nyra Sol has recorded the result

### Function
- Riven should remain visible in the guild hall as a named duelist
- his ambient speech should change based on whether the player won or lost
- he should feel like someone Nyra may call back for future formal challenges

### Player-Facing Outcome
- if the player wins:
  - Riven acknowledges the clean read
  - he makes it clear the assessment is over, not that anyone needed to die
  - he frames the duel as a real benchmark, not a scripted loss
  - he becomes a recognizable hall rival instead of a disposable trial opponent
- if the player loses:
  - Riven keeps the tone professional
  - he makes it clear the result is a recorded failure, not a death condition
  - he treats the result as a real rank-office record
  - he remains available as a future rematch-facing challenger

## Phase III: Later Guild Return

### Trigger
- later rank movement
- later hall access
- or a future duel circuit / sparring slice that needs a named adventurer opponent

### Function
- Riven can return as a hall-side benchmark for higher confidence climbs
- he can act as a living reminder that the player has already beaten or been beaten by a real guild competitor
- he should be usable later as:
  - a repeat duel contact
  - a sparring benchmark
  - a guild-recognition hook for later rank ceremonies

## Examiner Continuity

Nyra Sol is the official record-keeper for Riven's role in the promotion chain.
Nyra is also the person who should explicitly tell the player that the duel is sanctioned and nonlethal.

Nyra should:
- reference Riven as a sanctioned duelist, not as random battle filler
- describe the duel as a guild assessment rather than a fight to the death
- keep the promotion record language formal, not murderous
- remember whether the player beat him cleanly or struggled
- adjust her tone based on the result
- use Riven as a benchmark for later promotion talk when appropriate

Riven should never replace Nyra.
He is the duel face.
Nyra is the guild authority.
Both need to feel like parts of the same promotion circuit.
